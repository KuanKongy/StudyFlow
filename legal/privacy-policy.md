# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** March 18, 2026

**Last Updated:** March 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how StudyFlow collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information. By using StudyFlow, you consent to the practices described below.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### Personal Information (from Auth0)
- **Email address** — used for account identification.
- **Display name** — your real name or chosen display name.
- **Profile picture URL** — linked from your authentication provider.
- **Auth0 subject ID** — a pseudonymous identifier used internally to associate data with your account.

### User-Authored Content
- **Notes** — text content you write and save.
- **Flashcards** — question/answer pairs you create or that are generated by AI.
- **Summaries** — AI-generated summaries derived from your notes.

This content could contain personal information, including names, course information, and personal reflections. You are responsible for the content you enter.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- To provide the StudyFlow service: storing your study materials, managing group memberships, and enabling collaboration.
- To generate AI-powered summaries and flashcards when you explicitly request them.
- To authenticate your identity and secure your account.
- We do **not** use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than providing the StudyFlow service.

## 3. Data Storage and Encryption

- **Database:** MongoDB Atlas, deployed in ca-central-1 (Canada). Data is encrypted at rest using MongoDB Atlas Transparent Data Encryption (AES-256).
- **Cache and Queue:** Hosted Redis, configured in ca-central-1 with TLS encryption in transit.
- **CLOUD Act Disclosure:** MongoDB Atlas is operated by MongoDB, Inc., a US-based company. Despite data being stored in ca-central-1, US authorities could potentially compel access under the US CLOUD Act. We disclose this honestly.

## 4. Third-Party Processors

| Provider | Purpose | Data Shared | Location |
|----------|---------|-------------|----------|
| Auth0 | Authentication and identity | Email, name, profile picture | US-based |
| OpenAI | AI summary and flashcard generation | Note content (text) | US-based |

## 5. Data Residency

All persistent StudyFlow-managed storage (MongoDB Atlas and hosted Redis) is configured in ca-central-1 (Canada).

The sole exception is AI processing: when you use AI features, your note content is transmitted to OpenAI's API servers in the United States. This transit is disclosed before your first use of AI features and is documented in our Terms of Service.

## 6. Your Rights and Data Deletion

- **Access:** You can view all your data through the StudyFlow interface.
- **Deletion:** You can delete your account at any time. This triggers a full cascade:
  - All your notes, materials, flashcards, jobs, and group audit log entries are deleted from MongoDB.
  - All your Redis cache entries and queued jobs are purged.
  - Your membership is removed from all groups.
  - Your Auth0 identity is retained so you can sign in again later without repeating onboarding.
- **Limitation:** Data already sent to OpenAI for AI processing before your deletion cannot be recalled. OpenAI retains API call logs per their own retention policy. This is outside our control and is disclosed in the Terms of Service.

## 7. Cookies and Tokens

- **Auth0 JWT and refresh tokens** are stored in browser localStorage for session management.
- We do **not** use tracking cookies, analytics cookies, or any third-party tracking scripts.

## 8. Data Monetization

Your data is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose other than providing the StudyFlow service and the AI features you explicitly request. There is no advertising on StudyFlow.

## 9. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify affected users as promptly as possible with details of the breach and steps taken to mitigate it.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of StudyFlow after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
